entries; August 3 has 80. There are two connection-specific profile IDs plus the

processing. Notification routing is off. Duck suppression is enabled in AA but
not outside AA. Playback notification muting is enabled including outside AA.

These are historical captures through August 3, not a fresh October phone
backup. Do not automatically restore one over current on-device preferences.

recheck profile/route selection and permissions after install. Never assume
adb install -r can replace an APK signed with a different key.
